## Local statements and dependencies

- Lemma 1 proves IAP for a chain union whose founder set is finite.
  It does not assert that stagewise finiteness alone suffices.
- Theorems 2–3 supply two CA-free constraints and use Alexander's
  Theorem 13 to obtain initial clusters before applying Zorn's lemma.
- Theorem 4 uses Alexander's Objective Species Theorem to identify
  the represented infinite generator-equivalence classes. It does
  not identify all finite clusters or assert uniqueness of maximal
  representatives.
- Theorem 5 gives an explicit continuous reduction from row-finiteness;
  Appendix A proves completeness of that source set. Its complexity
  notation is boldface Borel, not an effective index-set assertion.
- Corollary 6 concerns an eventual binary decision, including
  noncomputable learners. It does not rule out every possible
  species-valued classification criterion.

The immigrant example in Section 4 uses reflection to force infinitely
many backbone descendants. This hypothesis is needed before IAP
excludes infinitely many immigrants. The argument does not prohibit
individual late founders or assert that every CA-free solution must
exclude them.

All proof dependencies are either written in the paper or attributed
to the published source. Earlier editions of entries 06, 10 and 11 are
preserved in their respective dated archives. No unpublished working
notes are needed to read the current paper.
