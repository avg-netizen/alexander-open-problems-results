### Mathematical presentation and scope

The species paper uses a common finite-founder chain lemma, states
exactly where Alexander's coverage and Objective Species theorems
are used, and treats the fixed-bound families in the infinite-class
comparison. It includes the continuous reduction and its
row-finiteness completeness argument. Borel complexity is
distinguished from an effective index-set assertion, and the
non-learning corollary concerns a binary decision.

The immigrant obstruction explicitly requires reflection before
excluding infinitely many immigrants. The paper makes no broad
claim that CA-free coverage requires excluding late founders.
Published source locations are recorded in
[06/SOURCES.md](06-founding-cohort-species/SOURCES.md).

The internodon paper gives the exact swap statement, including
root cases, and its clade-distance corollary. It identifies the
same-domain finite-graph hypotheses for imported merge-tree height
stability. It explains SD, uncovered organisms, finite windows,
sampling and every metric denominator. The dedicated co-member
sample is presented separately from the parent/clade sample.
See [08/SOURCES.md](08-internodon-order-robustness/SOURCES.md).

This is a substantive explanatory rewrite with clarified scopes,
not a claim that every formula or argument was preserved verbatim.
The existing simulation programs and saved records are unchanged;
the new figure script reads those records. No new biological
experiment, independent mathematical review, priority finding or
formal certificate has been added.

### Assumptions made explicit

The pseudo-visibility paper distinguishes a private exogenous query
from frozen shared randomness. Its convergence theorem specifies
independent episode sampling and conditionally unbiased bounded-variance
noise at adaptive visits, rather than inferring a law of large numbers
from stationary marginal means alone. Final-policy evaluation and
exploratory training reward remain separate.

The quitting paper includes the never-crossing case in the ultrafilter
proof, uses a rational equality witness at negative pathwise thresholds,
and distinguishes dominance from equality with signed pathwise rewards.

function-program convention: total base environment weight is bounded
by 2^ell, and m+ell simulation bits give residual budget 2^(-m).

are explicit. This is a proof expansion, not a formula-preservation claim.
