## What is proved locally

The manuscript proves the reward duality, value-negating injection with exact
convergence reflection, off-path kernel recoverability, finite inverse tests,
contraction and unique infinite backward core, adjacent-depth pairing,
ordinary partial-program realization of its two parity cases, universality
and prefix-freeness, and equality of shortest full-kernel descriptions.
The final Kraft bound and absolutely convergent cancellation use the original
kernel sum. No unproved private lemma or project label is a mathematical
dependency.

The *Biologically unavoidable sequences* exposition was consulted as an
editorial model. It is not used as a mathematical source for this result.
Earlier exploratory notes on fixed-prior intelligence sign keep their own
scope; this construction chooses the universal machine allowed by the source
question and does not classify every fixed symmetric machine.
